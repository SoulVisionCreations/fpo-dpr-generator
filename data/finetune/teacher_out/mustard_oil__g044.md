## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Sesame (til) oil is a premium edible oil prized for its distinctive nutty aroma, rich taste, and natural stability. Extracted from sesame seeds that are traditionally grown across peninsular India, the oil is known for its culinary versatility and cultural significance. For Kollipara Til Growers Producer Company Ltd, based in Kollipara, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, sesame oil offers an ideal avenue for value addition to member produce, enabling direct farm-to-market branding with full traceability.

The FPO proposes a hot expeller-based, filtered sesame oil unit. Crude oil is expressed from cleaned and conditioned seeds, then filtered and filled into consumer and institutional packs. The by-product, protein- and fiber-rich oil cake, is a valuable input for cattle and poultry feed formulations. Raw material is sourced primarily from FPO member farmers and supplemented through local markets to ensure continuity of operations and consistent quality.

### Uses & Benefits
- Culinary: Widely used for cooking, tempering, pickles, sweets, chutneys, podis, marinades, salad dressings, and bakery applications. Its robust flavor elevates traditional and contemporary recipes.
- Nutritional: Naturally rich in unsaturated fats and bioactive antioxidants such as sesamol and sesamin. It supports balanced diets when used alongside diverse cooking oils.
- Functional: Offers good oxidative stability, making it suitable for repeated kitchen use within safe practices. The characteristic aroma also allows lower dosages for flavoring.
- Traditional wellness: Employed in massage oils and topical applications within Ayurveda-inspired routines.
- Cultural: Preferred for festive cooking, temple offerings, and ceremonial lamps, adding to baseline demand beyond typical retail consumption.

### Market Potential
Demand for traditional edible oils is expanding as consumers rediscover regional tastes, authenticity, and traceable sourcing. Urban households increasingly seek minimally processed, filtered and cold-pressed options, while rural markets continue to prefer familiar flavors for everyday cooking. Andhra Pradesh and neighboring states exhibit strong culinary affinity to sesame oil, supported by thriving pickle, snack, and sweet sectors that require consistent quality.

Institutional buyers such as restaurants, caterers, pickle manufacturers, and specialty food brands provide steady off-take when assured of purity, flavor consistency, and reliable supply. E-commerce and direct-to-consumer channels further enable FPO-branded products to access wider geographies without heavy intermediation. With strategic positioning, Kollipara Til Growers Producer Company Ltd can service both premium niches and mass-traditional segments.

### Value Chain
- Farm-level aggregation: Member farmers adopt good agricultural practices, including timely harvest and hygienic post-harvest handling to limit impurities and prevent mold. Primary cleaning and safe drying at village level preserve seed quality.
- Procurement and grading: The FPO aggregates sesame seeds from members and local markets under transparent quality norms. Basic checks for moisture, foreign matter, and free fatty acids safeguard processing outcomes.
- Pre-processing: Seeds are sun-dried to stabilize moisture, then mechanically cleaned to remove dust, stones, and other impurities. Gentle heating/conditioning improves oil expressibility and enhances the nutritional profile of the cake.
- Oil extraction: An expeller press expresses crude oil from conditioned seeds. Process parameters are optimized for acceptable recovery while protecting flavor and color.
- Filtration and finishing: A filter press removes suspended particles to yield bright, clean oil. Inert gas flushing and food-grade handling may be used to protect aroma and retard oxidation.
- Packaging and storage: Oil is packed in food-grade bottles and containers with tamper-evident sealing. Proper labeling with FSSAI credentials, batch codes, and best-before dates assures compliance and consumer confidence. The cake is bagged and marketed to feed manufacturers and dairy farmers.
- Distribution: Multichannel sales through local kirana stores, modern retail, institutional buyers, and online platforms ensure reach and better price realization for members.

### Competition & Marketing
The competitive set includes national edible oil brands, established regional mills, imported products, and boutique cold-pressed labels. Winning propositions revolve around purity, taste, freshness, and provenance. The FPO can differentiate on:
- Farm-traceability and member ownership, offering authenticity and social impact.
- Freshly filtered batches with consistent flavor and aroma.
- Honest labelling, AGMARK grading (where opted), and FSSAI-compliant quality assurance.
- Tailored pack formats for households and food businesses.
- Relationship-based institutional sales and sampling-led consumer trials.
- Storytelling around Kollipara’s growers, local varieties, and traditional culinary culture.

Promotional avenues include chef partnerships, food festivals, temple and community events, and digital campaigns focused on recipes and nutrition. Value-added extensions such as roasted variants or blended culinary oils can be evaluated in subsequent phases based on market response.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material volatility: Seasonal swings and weather risks can disrupt supply and prices. Forward linkages with members, staggered procurement, scientific storage, and transparent pricing frameworks reduce exposure.
- Quality risks: High moisture and fungal contamination can degrade oil and cake. Strong SOPs for drying, cleaning, and testing for moisture and free fatty acids, along with supplier training and incentive alignment, are critical.
- Processing risks: Power outages, equipment downtime, and filtration bottlenecks can affect throughput. Adequate maintenance planning, spares inventory, operator training, and backup power arrangements enhance reliability.
- Market risks: Price competition and adulteration in the market can erode margins. Brand-building, authenticity claims backed by lab reports, and strategic channel partnerships can defend pricing.
- Regulatory and food safety: Non-compliance can disrupt operations. FSSAI licensing, periodic lab tests, labeling accuracy, and traceability digitalization keep the enterprise audit-ready.
- Financial risks: Working capital stress during peak procurement seasons can strain liquidity. Banking limits sized to the operating cycle, disciplined receivables management, and risk-sharing arrangements with buyers help smooth cash flows.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The project generates steady on-site jobs in cleaning, expelling, filtration, packaging, warehousing, sales, and administration, with strong potential for women’s participation in quality control and packaging lines. Member incomes rise through assured offtake and value capture within the FPO. Local dairies and feed manufacturers benefit from reliable supply of oil cake, supporting livestock productivity. Skills in quality assurance, maintenance, and food safety elevate the rural talent base and foster youth entrepreneurship in allied services.

### Sustainability
The expeller-based process is solvent-free and low on effluent generation. Oil cake is fully valorized as feed, minimizing waste. Energy-efficient motors, heat insulation on cookers, and consideration of solar-assisted drying or rooftop solar improve energy intensity. Hygienic, reusable, or recyclable packaging options and responsible disposal of used consumables align with extended producer responsibility. Traceability and climate-resilient agronomy, including soil health practices and moisture conservation, strengthen long-term ecological viability.
