## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Mango juice is a refreshing, fruit-based beverage produced from ripe, high-flavour mangoes through hygienic extraction, clarification, pasteurization, and packaging. For Betnoti Mango Growers FPO Ltd, Betnoti, Badasahi, Mayurbhanj, Odisha, this enterprise leverages abundant local mango availability and converts a seasonal, perishable crop into a safe, shelf-stable, branded product. The processing approach selected ensures consistent taste, colour, and aroma while meeting food safety standards. The formulation includes dilution to achieve the desired sensory profile, fine pulping and filtration for smooth mouthfeel, heat treatment for microbial stability, and the use of permitted preservatives such as potassium metabisulphite to protect flavour and extend shelf life.

### Uses & Benefits
- Convenient, ready-to-drink hydration suitable for households, on-the-go consumers, and institutional channels such as canteens, cafés, and hotels.
- A natural fruit-based option that provides the characteristic taste, aroma, and appeal of regional mango varieties.
- Year-round availability through pulp preservation and controlled processing, reducing seasonal supply disruption.
- Value addition for farmers by converting raw mangoes into a higher-value packaged beverage with stronger market reach.
- Reduction of post-harvest losses and improved income stability through aggregation and processing at source.
- Potential to cater to diverse preferences via differentiated pack sizes, sweetness levels, and branding narratives.

### Market Potential
Demand for fruit beverages continues to rise with growing urbanization, preference for convenient packaged drinks, and expanding modern retail and e-commerce. Mango is the most preferred fruit flavour across much of India, enjoying wide acceptance in both rural and urban markets. Odisha’s climate and cultural affinity for mango lend additional strength to local brand stories. Institutional and HoReCa channels, quick-service restaurants, tourist hubs, and educational establishments create sustained offtake. Regional positioning, coupled with traceability from an FPO, enhances credibility and can command a quality premium. There is also scope to explore nearby state markets and niche export opportunities where Indian mango flavour profiles are prized.

### Value Chain
- Farm level: Aggregation of ripe, varietally suitable mangoes; basic sorting and grading at collection points to minimize damage.
- Pre-processing: Washing, inspection, removal of stones and segments, followed by controlled dilution to formulation requirements.
- Processing: Mechanical pulping and fine filtration to achieve desired consistency; pasteurization for microbial safety; cooling; dosing of permitted preservative; final heat treatment; rapid cooling and hygienic storage.
- Packaging: Food-grade bottles or aseptic packs with proper coding and labelling; secondary packaging for transport.
- Storage & logistics: Ambient or chilled storage as required; efficient distribution to retailers, wholesalers, and institutions.
- By-products: Skins and stones can be channelled into feed, compost, pectin extraction, seed-kernel value streams, or bioenergy, minimizing waste and adding revenue.
- Quality & compliance: Adherence to FSSAI standards, robust HACCP-based controls, regular sensory and microbiological checks, and batch-wise traceability from farm to bottle.

### Competition & Marketing
Competition includes national beverage brands, regional processors, and local unorganized juice vendors. The FPO can differentiate through farm-origin storytelling, variety-led taste notes, clean-label positioning, and strict quality assurance. Marketing can emphasize freshness, traceability, and community impact. A multi-channel approach combining kirana stores, modern retail, institutional sales, and digital commerce helps de-risk demand. Visibility drivers include in-store tastings, POS materials, local festivals and fairs, tie-ups with food service, and social media outreach. Packaging aesthetics, easy-to-read labels, and recyclable materials enhance brand appeal and trust.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material variability: Mitigated through multi-variety sourcing, staggered procurement windows, and pulp preservation for off-season continuity.
- Weather and crop risk: Addressed via forward purchase agreements with member farmers, ripening protocols, and contingency procurement from nearby districts.
- Quality and food safety: Controlled by GMP and HACCP systems, strict sanitation, water treatment, calibrated thermal processing, and routine lab testing.
- Market price pressure: Managed through differentiated branding, pack-mix optimization, institutional contracts, and cost discipline across procurement and packaging.
- Operational downtime: Reduced via preventive maintenance, critical spares stocking, trained operators, and reliable utilities with backup power.
- Regulatory compliance: Ensured through up-to-date FSSAI licences, label conformity, and adherence to permissible additives and claims.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The unit creates local employment in processing, quality assurance, packaging, warehousing, and sales, with strong scope for women’s participation in sorting, bottling, and labelling. Farmer members gain assured offtake, better price realization, and dividends through ownership in the FPO. Skills development in hygiene, food safety, and equipment operation elevates the local talent base. Ancillary livelihoods in logistics, repairs, and materials supply further multiply the economic benefits within Mayurbhanj and neighbouring areas.

### Sustainability
- Raw material: Short supply chains reduce transport emissions; on-farm training promotes responsible agro-practices.
- Water and energy: Closed-loop cleaning, efficient CIP, and energy-optimized pasteurization lower resource intensity; renewable power integration improves the footprint.
- Waste: Valorization of peels and stones; composting and biogas options; proper effluent treatment for compliance.
- Packaging: Lightweight, recyclable formats; participation in extended producer responsibility frameworks; consumer education on disposal.
- Transport: Route planning, consolidated dispatches, and local market focus reduce fuel consumption and costs.
