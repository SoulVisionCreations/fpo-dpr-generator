## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Mango juice is a refreshing beverage prepared from ripe, flavourful mangoes. It captures the natural aroma, colour, and sweetness of the fruit in a stable, ready-to-drink format. For this project, East Khasi Hills Mango Producers Company Ltd, Mawphlang, East Khasi Hills, Meghalaya will aggregate member produce, process fresh fruit through washing, seed removal, pulping, clarification, pasteurization, and hygienic packaging, with appropriate use of permitted preservatives such as potassium metabisulphite to ensure safety and shelf stability. The product will be positioned as an authentic, farm-linked juice reflecting the terroir and biodiversity of Meghalaya.

### Uses & Benefits
- Convenient, ready-to-serve hydration suitable for households, schools, offices, and travel.
- Retains the characteristic mango taste, colour, and mouthfeel, appealing to all age groups.
- Fortifies daily diets with naturally occurring fruit nutrients, including carotenoids and vitamin-rich compounds present in mango.
- Enables consistent year-round availability despite the seasonal nature of mango harvests.
- Offers foodservice versatility across restaurants, cafés, hotels, canteens, and catering.

### Market Potential
Mango is one of India’s most loved fruits, and mango-based beverages enjoy strong, enduring demand. Urban retail, semi-urban trade, and institutional channels show steady offtake for safe and consistent fruit beverages. The North Eastern region offers an emerging consumer base, with Shillong and neighbouring urban hubs serving as anchor markets. Touristic footfall and transit corridors create additional seasonal surges, while e-commerce and quick-commerce platforms open access to digitally savvy consumers. With rising preference for hygienically processed, traceable, and trustworthy products, an FPO-backed mango juice from Meghalaya can credibly claim provenance and quality, supporting premium positioning where appropriate.

### Value Chain
- Farm production and aggregation: Member farmers harvest, grade, and bring produce to collection points. The FPO establishes simple, transparent procurement and quality acceptance protocols, with traceability retained to farm clusters.
- Primary processing: At the unit, mangoes are washed, destoned, and converted to pulp through hygienic pulping and finishing. Filtration and standardization ensure uniform texture and flavour.
- Thermal processing and preservation: Pasteurization ensures microbial safety. Potassium metabisulphite may be added within regulatory limits to extend shelf life while retaining sensory attributes.
- Packaging and storage: Juice is filled into food-grade containers, sealed, labelled, and lot-coded. Finished goods are cooled and stored under controlled conditions to maintain quality.
- Distribution: The FPO services a mix of channels, including local retail, modern trade, HORECA, institutions, and online marketplaces, supported by cold or cool-chain practices where relevant.
- By-product utilization: Peels and seeds are valorized for feed, composting, or potential extraction, reducing disposal costs and environmental impact.

### Competition & Marketing
The category features national brands and strong regional players. Differentiation for East Khasi Hills Mango Producers Company Ltd will rest on:
- Farm-origin storytelling, Meghalaya provenance, and FPO ownership that ensures fair value for farmers.
- Emphasis on authentic mango taste, consistent quality, and safe processing practices.
- Packaging that is attractive, informative, and eco-conscious, with clear labelling on ingredients and provenance.
- Multi-channel presence: general trade, modern retail, institutional buyers, and digital platforms.
- Strategic partnerships with local hospitality and tourism operators, plus community sampling and school outreach to build trust and familiarity.
- Seasonal limited editions by cultivar or taste notes, highlighting Meghalaya’s biodiversity.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material seasonality and weather risks: Mitigate via staggered sourcing windows, inter-state procurement agreements, and partial reliance on stored pulp when fresh fruit is scarce.
- Quality variability: Implement strict grading, incoming quality checks, and standardized processing SOPs with trained QA oversight.
- Food safety and compliance: Adopt robust sanitation, pest control, personnel hygiene, and batch traceability with documented recall preparedness.
- Utilities and equipment downtime: Ensure reliable power backup, preventive maintenance, and service contracts for critical machinery.
- Market acceptance and price pressure: Build brand equity with provenance-led marketing, diversified pack formats, and channel-specific promotions.
- Working capital tightness: Align credit terms with distributors, maintain adequate inventory controls, and utilize appropriate banking limits.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The project creates steady livelihood opportunities for local youth and women in processing, quality assurance, packaging, warehousing, and sales. Member farmers benefit from assured procurement, transparent pricing, and reduced post-harvest losses. Capacity-building on good agricultural and post-harvest practices further strengthens the local ecosystem. Surpluses generated by the FPO can be reinvested into community assets, farmer services, and business expansion.

### Sustainability
Sustainability will be embedded through efficient water use and recycling, responsible handling of effluents, and energy-saving utilities. Fruit waste will be converted to value, reducing landfill and creating circular benefits. Preference will be given to recyclable or biodegradable packaging and to suppliers who meet ethical and environmental standards. Over time, the unit can explore renewable energy and carbon-reduction initiatives to further lower its footprint.
