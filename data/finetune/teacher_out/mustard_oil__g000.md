## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Dongargarh Til Utpadak Farmer Producer Company, Dongargarh, Rajnandgaon, Chhattisgarh proposes a community-owned sesame oil enterprise that converts members’ sesame seeds into premium edible oil and value-added by-products. Sesame oil is a traditional, flavourful cooking medium prized for its nutty aroma, thermal stability, and natural antioxidative constituents that help maintain quality during storage and cooking. The proposed unit will follow a robust primary processing sequence: seed drying to achieve safe moisture, cleaning to remove impurities, controlled seed heating to enhance pressing performance, mechanical expeller extraction for oil recovery, and filtration to achieve clear, bright oil prior to filling and packaging. The by-product, pressed sesame oil cake, will be aggregated and sold into the cattle-feed and compound feed market.

Raw material for this project is clean, mature sesame seed sourced from FPO member farmers and local growers, handled carefully to avoid contamination and moisture ingress. Typical procurement will include white and other local varieties preferred in regional cuisine and milling. Quality attention centres on moisture, soundness of seed, and absence of live or dead insect matter, aflatoxin-prone lots, stones, and extraneous material. Seeds are received, lot-wise sampled, dried in yards or solar-assisted dryers, and stored in ventilated conditions prior to processing.

### Uses & Benefits
- Culinary uses across household and institutional kitchens for sautéing, tempering, deep-frying, and cold applications such as salads, chutneys, and pickles.
- Ingredient in bakery, confectionery, and ready-to-eat foods, imparting distinctive flavour and sheen.
- Recognised in traditional wellness, massage, and personal care applications due to its emollient and antioxidative properties.
- Natural preservatives in sesame contribute to oxidative stability, helping reduce rancidity and extending sensory quality.
- By-product oil cake provides a protein- and mineral-rich input for cattle and small ruminant feed formulations.

### Market Potential
Demand for minimally processed, traceable edible oils is rising with consumer preference for authentic taste, perceived healthfulness, and local sourcing. In central and eastern Indian markets, sesame oil retains strong culinary linkages in festive foods, pickles, and speciality cuisines, with widening adoption in urban wellness segments and gourmet retail. Institutional buyers in snacks, condiments, and bakery value the oil’s flavour and heat stability. Digital commerce and modern retail formats are expanding market reach beyond traditional kirana channels, while growing interest in farmer-led brands enhances trust and willingness to trial. The steady feed demand for pressed cake adds resilience to revenue, supporting year-round utilisation.

### Value Chain
FPO members cultivate sesame and deliver produce to village collection points for primary grading and moisture check. The hub receives lots, undertakes drying to safe storage levels, and performs cleaning and de-stoning. Conditioned seeds undergo gentle heating or roasting to improve pressing behaviour and enhance aroma, after which the mechanical expeller extracts crude oil. A filter press clarifies the oil, which then moves to a hygienic filling line for bottles, pouches, or tins, sealed and batch-coded for traceability. Pressed cake is cooled, bagged, and dispatched to feed blenders and dairies. The FPO anchors services such as farmer training, quality testing, transparent weighment, and payment settlement. Forward linkages include wholesale distributors, modern trade, food processors, institutional canteens, and e-commerce fulfilment.

### Competition & Marketing
Competition comes from refined blended oils, large branded sesame oils, and a fast-growing cohort of artisanal or cold-pressed labels. The FPO’s differentiation rests on farm-to-bottle traceability, assured purity, regional identity, and consistent flavour. Branding can emphasise seed provenance, gentle processing, and independent quality checks under food safety norms. A multi-channel approach covers general trade, modern retail, and direct-to-consumer platforms, with institutional packs for kitchens and processors. Sampling at local fairs and festivals, chef partnerships, and nutrition-led content marketing will drive awareness. Trade schemes aligned to channel margins, returnable containers for bulk buyers, and festive hampers can support seasonal uplift.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material volatility and quality risks: Mitigated by staggered procurement, pre-harvest engagement with agronomy support, moisture management, and strict incoming quality protocols including aflatoxin screening for suspect lots.
- Processing and equipment downtime: Addressed through preventive maintenance, annual service contracts, critical spares inventory, and operator training.
- Food safety and adulteration concerns: Controlled with documented SOPs, supplier declarations, batch-wise records, sealed dispatches, and periodic third-party lab tests.
- Market and price competition: Managed via differentiated branding, membership-backed cost advantage, diversified pack formats, and by-product realisation.
- Working capital constraints: Eased through banking lines, inventory planning, and forward supply agreements with anchor buyers.
- Power and utility interruptions: Mitigated by backup power arrangements and energy-efficient equipment specification.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The enterprise creates local jobs in drying yards, cleaning, pressing, filtration, packaging, quality assurance, logistics, and sales, with roles accessible to women and youth. Member farmers benefit from better price discovery, prompt payment, dividend potential, and buy-back of graded produce. Skills development in post-harvest handling, moisture control, hygiene, and record-keeping elevates farm-gate value. Local dairies and feed mills gain a dependable cake supply, strengthening allied livelihoods and keeping value addition within the district.

### Sustainability
Sesame oil milling is inherently low-waste, with pressed cake fully utilised in feed. Emphasis on sun or solar-assisted drying cuts thermal energy needs and reduces spoilage. Minimal water use, proper effluent handling from washdowns, and safe solid waste disposal uphold environmental compliance. Use of recyclable packaging, options for bulk refill in institutions, and participation in extended producer responsibility programmes enhance circularity. Natural antioxidants in sesame reduce the need for synthetic additives, supporting clean-label positioning.
