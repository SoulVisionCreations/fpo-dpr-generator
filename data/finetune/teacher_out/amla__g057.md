## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
This Detailed Project Report presents a litchi juice value addition unit for Sualkuchi Horti Producers FPC Ltd, Sualkuchi, Hajo, Kamrup, Assam. Litchi is a fragrant, delicate fruit with translucent arils prized for their floral sweetness. The juice captures these sensory notes while delivering a refreshing, premium beverage aligned with rising demand for natural fruit-based drinks. The FPO’s proximity to litchi-growing clusters in Assam provides reliable access to fresh produce, supports farm-gate value addition, and strengthens local livelihoods.

Within the processing line, fresh litchi is washed, de-seeded, separated into segments, diluted, pulped, filtered, gently pasteurised, cooled, stabilised with permitted preservatives such as potassium metabisulphite, pasteurised again, and then cooled and stored prior to filling. The resulting product can be packed as juice, nectar, or beverage bases across multiple formats suitable for retail, Horeca, and institutional buyers.

### Uses & Benefits
- Refreshing beverage with distinctive aroma and taste, appealing to both mainstream and premium consumers.
- Versatile ingredient for mocktails, desserts, dairy blends, ice lollies, smoothies, and artisanal beverages.
- Supports off-season consumption of litchi through safe thermal processing and preservation.
- Promotes farmer incomes by converting a highly perishable fruit into a stable, value-added product.
- Enhances rural enterprise and women’s participation via sorting, grading, bottling, and quality control roles.
- Facilitates consistent product quality and food safety through controlled processing and packaging.

### Raw Material
The primary raw material is ripe, fresh litchi sourced directly from member orchards and nearby growers. Desired attributes include intact skin, adequate maturity, and aromatic, firm arils. Secondary inputs include potable process water, permitted acidity regulators, sweeteners as per recipe, potassium metabisulphite for preservation within regulatory limits, and cleaning and sanitation materials. Packaging materials typically include PET bottles, glass bottles, caps, aseptic pouches or cartons, labels, and secondary corrugated packaging. Utilities encompass clean water, food-grade steam or hot water for pasteurisation, compressed air, electricity, and cold storage where applicable.

### Market Potential
India’s fruit beverage market is expanding with a clear shift toward authentic fruit content, regional flavours, and traceable sourcing. Litchi, though seasonal, commands strong consumer recall and is often viewed as a premium flavour across packaged drinks, confectionery, frozen desserts, and culinary applications. Assam’s identity and biodiversity add a regional storytelling advantage, supporting differentiated positioning in urban and tourist markets, as well as within the Northeast. Institutional buyers, cafes, quick-service restaurants, and e-commerce channels offer additional routes to market. With appropriate branding and quality assurance, the FPO can place litchi juice alongside established fruit lines and seasonal limited-edition offerings, enabling attractive margins.

### Value Chain
- Farm level: Aggregation through the FPO enables coordinated harvests, rapid transport to the unit, and reduced post-harvest losses. Primary grading at collection points helps maintain quality.
- Pre-processing: Washing, sorting, and careful de-seeding reduce contamination risks and preserve flavour. Traceability is maintained from orchard to batch.
- Processing: Controlled dilution, pulping, filtration, pasteurisation, and stabilisation safeguard sensory attributes and food safety. Adherence to Good Manufacturing Practices and documented sanitation procedures underpin compliance.
- Packaging & storage: Hot-fill systems or equivalent are used depending on pack type. Coded batches, secondary packaging, and recommended storage conditions ensure shelf stability and logistics efficiency.
- Distribution: Sales through local retail, supermarkets, Horeca, online platforms, institutional buyers, and government procurement routes diversify revenue. Seasonal stock planning and pulp storage enable steadier supplies to the market across the year.
- By-products: Skins and seeds can be directed to composting or explored for extraction and valorisation, enhancing sustainability and cost offsets.

### Competition & Marketing
Competition includes national fruit beverage brands, regional juice makers, imported drinks, and on-premise freshly prepared beverages. The FPO can differentiate with:
- Orchard-to-bottle traceability, farmer ownership, and Assam provenance.
- Clean-label positioning with permitted additives, consistent taste, and reliable quality.
- Seasonal storytelling, limited editions, and pairing with local cuisine and tourism.
- Focused channels such as regional retail chains, cafes, hotels, and event catering.
- Digital-first strategies using e-commerce listings, social media content, and hyperlocal delivery partners.
- Thoughtful packaging design, batch coding transparency, and food safety certifications to build trust.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Seasonality and perishability: Mitigated by staggered harvests, swift intake, pre-cooling, and pulp storage for extended packing schedules.
- Quality variability: Addressed via field-level training, harvest indices, procurement standards, and batch-wise laboratory checks.
- Microbiological and food safety risks: Managed through robust sanitation, validated thermal processing, preservative dosing controls, and documented standard operating procedures.
- Input price volatility: Reduced through forward procurement, supplier framework agreements, and packaging material planning.
- Power and utility interruptions: Countered by backup power, water storage, and equipment with thermal and energy efficiency features.
- Market fluctuations: Diversified SKUs, multi-channel sales, and brand-building reduce demand shocks; institutional contracts smooth volumes.
- Regulatory and compliance: Regular audits, staff training, and well-maintained records sustain compliance and buyer confidence.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The unit creates local employment in sorting, processing, packing, warehousing, logistics, sales, and administration, with significant opportunities for women and youth. Farmer members benefit from assured procurement, transparent pricing frameworks, and reduced wastage. Local services—transporters, packaging suppliers, maintenance contractors—gain steady business. Skills development in food safety and operations enhances long-term employability and lays the foundation for allied product lines such as mixed fruit beverages and pulp concentrates.

### Sustainability
- Raw material efficiency through careful de-seeding, pulping, and filtration to improve recovery and reduce waste.
- Water stewardship with reuse in non-product areas and responsible effluent management.
- Energy efficiency via insulated pipelines, heat recovery, and efficient motors; gradual adoption of renewable energy where feasible.
- By-product valorisation through composting or extraction use-cases for peels and seeds.
- Environmentally conscious packaging, recycling partnerships, and lighter secondary packaging where possible.
- Short supply chains that reduce transport emissions by sourcing from nearby orchards under the FPO umbrella.
