## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Subansiri Millet Farmers Producer Company Ltd., Doimukh, Papum Pare, Arunachal Pradesh proposes to set up an FPO-owned wheat flour (atta) mill to serve household and institutional demand for hygienically milled, branded atta. The unit will procure wheat grain from farmers and mandis, mill it through a modern line comprising cleaning, destoning, gravity separation, conditioning, milling, sieving, and automated form-fill-seal packing. The product aligns with evolving consumer preferences toward consistent quality, safe handling, and convenient packs. Stable raw material availability and storability enable round-the-year operations suited to an FPO enterprise.

### Uses & Benefits
- Daily staple for chapati, paratha, puri, and a wide range of traditional and contemporary foods.
- Consistent granulation and blending offer better cooking performance, aroma, and texture.
- Hygienic, tamper-evident packaging extends shelf life and protects against dust, pests, and moisture.
- Fortification-ready and suitable for multigrain variants to meet nutrition and taste preferences.
- By-product bran supports allied value chains such as cattle feed, bakeries, and nutraceuticals.

### Market Potential
Branded atta has grown rapidly as consumers shift from unorganized grinding to packaged offerings promising safety, purity, and consistency. Urbanizing clusters around Doimukh, Itanagar, Naharlagun, and adjoining districts show rising demand from households, eateries, hostels, and institutions. Limited presence of local organized mills in Arunachal Pradesh, dependence on inward supplies, and growing modern retail create a clear space for a reliable FPO brand with strong local roots. The unit can also target neighbouring districts and inter-state markets where logistics are favorable.

### Value Chain
- Procurement: Wheat grain sourced from member producers where feasible and from regional mandis to ensure quality, price efficiency, and volume continuity. Packaging materials procured from certified vendors.
- Primary processing: Multi-stage cleaning using vibro separation, destoning, and gravity separation to remove impurities; conditioning to achieve ideal tempering; controlled milling for optimal extraction; sieving and grading for consistency.
- Packaging and storage: Automated filling into consumer and bulk packs with batch coding; scientific warehousing with pallets and FIFO practices.
- Logistics and distribution: Direct retail servicing, wholesaler tie-ups, partnerships with local transporters, and digital order capture to optimize routes and coverage.
- Market linkages: Kirana stores, supermarkets, Horeca, institutions, and government or cooperative channels where eligible.

### Competition & Marketing
Competition includes national brands, regional players, and local chakki operators. The FPO will differentiate through traceable sourcing, freshness from shorter supply chains, and community trust. Marketing will focus on:
- Authentic, bilingual packaging communicating origin, hygiene, and taste attributes.
- In-store visibility, shopboard branding, sampling drives, and festival-led promotions.
- Institutional supply contracts to stabilize volumes and improve asset utilization.
- Digital engagement with hyperlocal delivery partners and community platforms.
- Emphasis on quality assurance, including regular lab checks and food safety compliance.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material price volatility: Diversified sourcing, staggered procurement, and forward arrangements reduce exposure; by-product sales provide a natural hedge.
- Quality variability: Standard operating procedures for grain grading and conditioning, robust lab testing, and supplier qualification norms ensure consistent output.
- Power and operational disruptions: Backup systems, preventive maintenance, and trained operators sustain uptime.
- Logistics constraints in hilly terrain and monsoon: Route planning, buffer inventories of key SKUs, and resilient packaging mitigate delays and damage.
- Market competition: Focused brand positioning, reliable service levels, and institutional contracts build defensibility.
- Regulatory and food safety: Strict adherence to licensing, labeling, and hygiene protocols, with documented traceability.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The project generates direct jobs in plant operations, quality control, packaging, logistics, and sales, with inclusive roles for women and local youth. It ensures dependable market access for farmers, strengthens the FPO’s bargaining power, and retains value addition within the district. Stable offtake and transparent payments enhance producer confidence, while consumer access to safe, affordable atta improves food security.

### Sustainability
Dry milling is resource-efficient, with low water intensity. Energy-efficient motors, dust extraction and filtration, and sound enclosures improve safety and reduce emissions. Segregated waste handling, recyclable packaging options, and optimized distribution routes cut environmental footprint. Bran and other by-products are fully valorized, minimizing losses and supporting circularity in local agri-ecosystems.
