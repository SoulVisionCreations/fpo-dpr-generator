## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Sesame oil, known locally as til ka tel, is an aromatic, golden edible oil pressed from mature sesame seeds. It is prized for its deep, nutty flavor, natural antioxidants, and culinary versatility across Indian cuisines. The oil’s inherent oxidative stability comes from unique lignans and phenolic compounds that also contribute to its characteristic aroma and color.

For Pandhurna Til Utpadak Producer Company Ltd., based in Pandhurna, Chhindwara, Madhya Pradesh, sesame oil offers a strong fit with local agro-ecology and farmer capabilities. The FPO aggregates clean, well-dried sesame seed lots from member farmers, enabling consistent quality and traceability from farm to bottle. Raw materials include graded sesame seeds with suitable moisture and purity, along with food-grade packaging such as PET, glass, or tins, closures, labels, and cartons.

Processing follows a traditional-yet-mechanized sequence: sun drying to optimize seed moisture; mechanical cleaning to remove stones, dust, and foreign matter; gentle heating or conditioning to enhance oil release and improve oil-cake quality; mechanical expeller pressing; filtration to achieve clear, bright oil; and hygienic filling and sealing. The pressed oil cake is a valuable by-product that finds assured offtake in cattle-feed and small ruminant feed formulations.

### Uses & Benefits
- Culinary: Widely used for tadka, pickles, sweets, snacks, marinades, and regional delicacies, sesame oil imparts a robust, nutty note and holds up well to sautéing and tempering.
- Nutrition and wellness: Naturally rich in unsaturated fats and antioxidants, sesame oil supports balanced diets when consumed in moderation. It is traditionally valued in Ayurveda for massage and wellness rituals.
- Bakery and confectionery: Preferred for specialty items that benefit from its distinctive aroma and stability.
- Industrial and artisanal: Employed in select personal care formulations, artisanal soaps, and traditional remedies.
- By-product advantage: Protein- and fiber-rich oil cake is a dependable input in compound feed, contributing to circular resource use and added revenue streams.

### Market Potential
Demand for authentic, minimally processed edible oils is expanding across urban and semi-urban India. Consumers increasingly seek purity, provenance, and traditional flavors, driving uptake of sesame oil in retail, food service, and ethnic specialty segments. Packaged, traceable FPO-branded oil can command trust with clean labeling, adherence to food safety norms, and consistent organoleptic quality.

Madhya Pradesh and neighboring states provide a ready supply base and a growing market via general trade, modern retail, and e-commerce. Institutional buyers such as snack manufacturers, pickle units, bakeries, sweet makers, and HORECA operators require dependable quality and deliveries, creating scope for longer-term supply relationships. The by-product oil cake strengthens the revenue model through steady B2B feed sales.

### Value Chain
Farm-level: Member farmers cultivate sesame and undertake basic post-harvest practices such as cleaning and drying. The FPO provides aggregation, fair grading, extension services, and assured procurement with transparent quality norms.

Primary processing: Seeds are dried to optimal moisture, cleaned, conditioned by controlled heating, and pressed in a mechanical expeller. The crude oil is filtered in stages to achieve clarity and shelf stability, then transferred to food-grade tanks.

Packaging and storage: The oil is filled into consumer and institutional packs, sealed, batch-coded, labeled, and packed into cartons. Finished goods are stored in a clean, cool area away from direct sunlight. The oil cake is bagged and stored separately under dry conditions.

Distribution: Sales flow through multiple channels—local kirana and wholesaler networks, modern retail, institutional buyers, and digital storefronts. Periodic third-party testing, adherence to FSSAI standards, and lot-wise traceability reinforce brand trust.

Support services: Preventive maintenance, calibrated weighing and filling, good manufacturing practices, and product liability insurance underpin quality assurance and risk management.

### Competition & Marketing
The market includes large national brands, regional packers, and local ghani units. Differentiation for the FPO will center on purity, traceability, and the farmer-owned brand story. Key strategies include:
- Positioning around authenticity, unadulterated quality, and farm-to-bottle transparency.
- Offering multiple pack formats for retail and bulk buyers.
- Building tie-ups with sweet makers, snack units, and pickle manufacturers for stable offtake.
- Leveraging in-store tastings, regional food festivals, chef collaborations, and digital storytelling.
- Ensuring strong last-mile service with reliable delivery and responsive after-sales support.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material quality variability: Addressed through clear procurement specs, on-site moisture checks, improved drying, and pre-shipment inspection.
- Price volatility: Mitigated via staggered procurement, buffer stocks, and diversified channel mix.
- Contaminants and food safety: Managed through robust supplier vetting, cleaning, filtration, and periodic accredited lab testing aligned to FSSAI norms.
- Equipment downtime and power fluctuations: Minimized with preventive maintenance, OEM service contracts, and backup power arrangements.
- Working capital stress: Eased by disciplined inventory cycles, established credit policies, and optimized drawdown of bank limits.
- Competitive pressures: Countered by brand building, value-added packaging, and consistent quality differentiation.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The project generates local employment across aggregation, processing, quality control, packaging, warehousing, logistics, and sales. Women’s participation is encouraged in grading, packing, and quality functions. Members benefit from better price realization, shared infrastructure, training in post-harvest handling, and potential patronage bonuses. Local feed markets gain from reliable oil-cake supply, strengthening dairy and livestock value chains.

### Sustainability
The model promotes resource efficiency by valorizing both oil and cake, minimizing waste. Sun drying reduces energy intensity in pre-processing. Efficient motors, heat recovery where feasible, and well-insulated equipment lower energy use. Food-grade packaging with a pathway for extended producer responsibility, rainwater harvesting, and responsible effluent handling support environmental compliance. Agronomic advisory on soil health, residue management, and integrated pest practices can further enhance sustainability and climate resilience.
