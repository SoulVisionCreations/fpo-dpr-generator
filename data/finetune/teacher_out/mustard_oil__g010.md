## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
Sesame oil, locally known as til oil, is a premium edible oil extracted from the seeds of the sesame plant. It is prized for its rich, nutty aroma, natural antioxidants, and culinary versatility. For Rosera Til Utpadak Producer Company Ltd, Samastipur, Bihar, sesame oil presents an opportunity to convert locally grown seeds into a high-value, branded product with clear differentiation based on provenance, purity, and traditional processing.

The raw material is cleaned and graded sesame seed sourced from farmer members and local growers. Seed quality drives oil flavour, yield, and stability, so strict attention is given to varietal selection, post-harvest handling, and moisture control. Clean, adequately dried seed with uniform colour and minimal impurities is essential. The by-product, oil cake (pina), is a valuable input for cattle and small ruminant feed formulations, providing an added revenue stream and circularity within the local agri-system.

The production method proposed follows a traditional expeller-pressing route enhanced by good manufacturing practices: sun drying to optimise moisture, mechanical cleaning to remove dust and admixture, controlled seed heating to facilitate oil release and enhance aroma, pressing in a screw expeller, filtration through a filter press to achieve bright appearance and stability, and hygienic filling and sealing. The process preserves the characteristic flavour and functional properties sought by household and institutional buyers alike.

### Uses & Benefits
- Culinary use across Indian cuisines for tempering, frying, pickles, namkeens, sweets, and specialty dishes, valued for its distinctive taste and aroma.
- Functional properties that support texture, mouthfeel, and flavour retention in traditional snacks and condiments.
- Natural antioxidants and lignans contribute to oxidative stability, assisting with shelf life under proper storage.
- Personal care and wellness applications in massage oils and hair oils, where purity and aroma are key.
- By-product oil cake serves as a protein- and energy-bearing ingredient in compound cattle feed, aiding feed manufacturers and dairy farmers.

### Market Potential
Sesame oil commands a loyal consumer base in multiple regional cuisines and among health-conscious urban households seeking minimally processed edible oils. Demand also stems from sweet makers, snack processors, pickle manufacturers, and HORECA segments that depend on authentic flavour and consistent quality. Bihar and adjoining states present ready markets through wholesale mandis, kirana stores, and modern retail, while e-commerce and direct-to-consumer subscriptions expand reach into metros and diaspora communities. With rising preference for traceable, farmer-owned brands, an FPO-led label from Rosera can occupy a differentiated space focused on purity, transparency, and origin.

### Value Chain
- Farm production: Member farmers cultivate sesame under rainfed and irrigated systems. Post-harvest, pods are dried and seeds are threshed, cleaned locally, and aggregated.
- Aggregation and procurement: The FPO undertakes fair and transparent procurement, incentivising clean seed with the right moisture and minimal admixture, and supporting farmers with tarpaulins and moisture management training.
- Primary processing: Seeds are sun-dried as needed, mechanically cleaned to remove dust, stones, weed seeds, and light impurities, and then gently heated or conditioned to aid oil release and develop the signature nutty aroma. 
- Oil extraction and finishing: Expeller pressing yields crude oil and oil cake; oil is settled and filtered through a filter press to achieve clarity and stability; finished oil is filled, sealed, batch-coded, and packed in food-grade containers; cake is bagged for the feed market.
- Distribution and sales: Finished oil moves to wholesale distributors, retail outlets, institutional buyers, and e-commerce channels; cake moves to feed mills and dairy clusters. Traceability, batch documentation, and food safety compliance are maintained throughout.

### Competition & Marketing
The category competes with refined multi-seed oils as well as other filtered traditional oils. Within sesame, buyers differentiate among filtered expeller oil for everyday cooking, toasted-aroma oils for specialty cuisines, and premium cold-pressed offerings in niche urban markets. The FPO can compete on authenticity, farmer ownership, transparent sourcing, and freshness from shorter supply chains.

Marketing strategy can blend local brand building in Samastipur and neighbouring districts with regional distribution through wholesale networks. Positioning themes include origin-based storytelling, purity assurance, and community benefit. Packaging design should highlight farmer ownership, unblended quality, and traditional expeller processing. BTL efforts with chefs, halwais, snack and pickle makers, and dairy-linked feed users for cake can help seed institutional demand. Digital storefronts, social media, and partnerships with rural retail points can deepen market reach.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material variability: Seed moisture, admixture, and storage conditions affect oil quality. Mitigation includes pre-procurement testing, moisture management support to farmers, grading-linked pricing, and proper godown practices.
- Price volatility: Sesame seed prices can swing with domestic harvests and trade dynamics. Forward procurement planning, buffer stocks, and staggered purchasing reduce exposure.
- Quality and contamination risks: Dust, stones, and microbial load can affect safety and shelf life. Investing in cleaning, hygienic handling, filtration, and food safety SOPs helps maintain standards.
- Operational downtime: Machinery breakdowns can disrupt output. Preventive maintenance, AMC support, and critical spares inventory limit stoppages.
- Market acceptance: New brands face slow uptake. Sampling, influencer kitchens, institutional onboarding, and satisfaction guarantees can accelerate adoption.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The enterprise generates skilled and semi-skilled jobs across seed handling, machine operation, filtration and packing, quality control, warehousing, sales, and administration. It creates steady offtake for member farmers, improves farm-gate realisations through direct procurement, and returns value via patronage bonuses and dividends as feasible. Women’s self-help groups can be engaged in cleaning, packing, and retail outreach, adding inclusive employment. The sale of oil cake to local dairies aligns with state priorities on livestock productivity and rural incomes.

### Sustainability
Sesame is well-suited to low-input systems and diversified crop rotations, supporting soil health and water-use efficiency. The processing line minimises waste: husk and fines can be repurposed, and oil cake re-enters the local feed economy. Energy-efficient motors, heat optimisation in conditioning, and potential adoption of rooftop solar can reduce the carbon footprint. Food-grade packaging, responsible waste management, and adherence to food safety systems ensure environmental compliance and consumer trust.
