## 2. Product, Market & Value Chain

### Introduction
This Detailed Project Report covers an integrated edible oil venture for Vanthali Groundnut Producers Company Ltd, Vanthali, Junagadh, Gujarat, focusing on filtered expeller-pressed groundnut oil. Groundnut oil, also known as peanut oil or mungfali tel, is a traditional Indian cooking medium prized for its clean, nutty aroma, stable frying performance, and compatibility with diverse regional cuisines. The proposed FPO-owned facility leverages the Saurashtra region’s strong groundnut base, translating member harvests into branded, value-added oil and a nutritious oil-cake by-product.

Raw material for this unit is clean, mature groundnut seed procured directly from member farmers and allied clusters. Post-harvest drying to safe moisture, careful cleaning, and conditioning are essential to preserve oil quality, maintain characteristic flavor, and protect functional properties during expeller extraction and filtration.

### Uses & Benefits
- Culinary: Widely used for sautéing, deep-frying, tadka, and confectionery applications due to its pleasant flavor and thermal stability. Its sensory profile complements traditional snacks, farsan, mithai, and everyday household cooking.
- Nutritional and sensory attributes: Recognized for a balanced fatty acid profile and naturally occurring antioxidants that support desirable taste, aroma, and oxidative stability when handled correctly.
- Industrial and artisanal uses: Serves as a base oil in select bakery, snack, and foodservice operations; also used in limited personal care, massage, and artisanal applications because of its light texture and skin-friendly feel.
- By-product utilization: Protein- and fiber-rich groundnut oil cake is a valuable cattle-feed ingredient for the dairy and livestock sector, strengthening circularity and boosting enterprise viability.

### Market Potential
Groundnut oil retains a strong consumer preference in western India and is steadily gaining traction in urban centers where customers seek authentic, traceable, and minimally processed oils. Growing awareness of origin-linked quality, the rise of regional brands, and willingness to pay for purity and flavor underpin demand. Institutional buyers in snacks, sweets, and foodservice value consistent quality and a reliable supply chain. An FPO brand that assures farm-level traceability, fair procurement, and credible quality systems is well positioned against commodity-grade alternatives and imported blends.

### Value Chain
The value chain begins with member farmers cultivating groundnut, supported by good agronomic practices and primary cleaning at the village level. At the processing unit:
- Seeds are sun-dried to bring down surface moisture and improve storability.
- Cleaning and destoning remove dust, husk, shriveled kernels, and foreign matter.
- Gentle heating or conditioning enhances oil release in the expeller and improves the functional quality of the resultant oil cake.
- Mechanical expeller pressing extracts crude oil without chemical solvents.
- A filter press clarifies the oil to achieve a bright, stable appearance and a clean taste profile.
- The oil is then filled, sealed, and packaged in food-grade bottles or tins, labeled for retail and institutional markets.
- Oil cake is collected, cooled, and bagged for sale as a cattle-feed ingredient to local dairies and feed mills.

Quality assurance spans raw material grading, moisture and impurity control, sensory checks, and periodic third-party lab testing for safety and compliance. Robust warehousing practices, first-in-first-out stock rotation, and hygienic handling preserve product integrity through distribution to wholesalers, retailers, e-commerce channels, and foodservice buyers.

### Competition & Marketing
The product competes with other edible oils and with branded and unbranded groundnut oil offerings. Key differentiators for the FPO are origin authenticity, freshness, transparent procurement, and farmer-owned value addition. Marketing will emphasize purity, traceability, and regional pride, with:
- Distinctive branding, tamper-evident packaging, and informative labels highlighting farm-to-bottle narratives.
- Multichannel distribution across general trade, modern retail, e-commerce, and direct institutional sales.
- In-store sampling, collaborations with snack makers and sweet shops, festival-time promotions, and digital storytelling to build trust and repeat purchase.
- Assurance seals, batch-wise traceability, and consistent sensory quality to command a brand premium over unorganized competitors.

### Risk & Mitigation
- Raw material volatility: Tackle through staggered procurement, pre-harvest tie-ups with members, quality-linked pricing, and safe storage protocols.
- Quality and food safety: Enforce rigorous sorting, moisture control, aflatoxin risk management, and validated sanitation procedures with regular lab testing.
- Operational reliability: Maintain preventive maintenance and annual service contracts for expellers and filters, and ensure standby power arrangements.
- Market and price pressure: Strengthen brand equity, optimize pack-mix, leverage by-product revenues, and cultivate institutional accounts for stable offtake.
- Compliance and reputation: Adhere to applicable food safety regulations and implement internal audits, recall preparedness, and insurance coverage.
- Climate and storage risks: Invest in ventilated warehouses, pallets, and pest management; adopt sun-drying best practices to protect kernel quality.

### Employment & Socio-Economic Impact
The project anchors stable offtake for member farmers, reduces distress sales, and channels more value to the producer community. It creates local jobs in operations, quality assurance, packaging, logistics, and administration, with a strong role for women in sorting, labeling, and finishing. Oil cake sales support the regional dairy economy, while skill development and entrepreneurial opportunities around distribution and retailing foster inclusive rural growth.

### Sustainability
The operation is intrinsically circular: mechanical extraction avoids chemical solvents, and oil cake is fully utilized in feed. Sun drying, energy-efficient motors, and the option of agro-residue or electric heating reduce the carbon footprint. Water use is minimal and limited to sanitation, with strict effluent and waste management practices. Recyclable packaging and responsible disposal under extended producer responsibility strengthen environmental stewardship. Local sourcing and short transport corridors further cut emissions while reinforcing transparent, resilient supply chains.
