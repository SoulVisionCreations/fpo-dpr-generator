### Introduction to the Product

Aonla (Amla / Indian gooseberry, Emblica officinalis) juice is a value-added, antioxidant-rich beverage; aonla is the richest natural source of vitamin C and is widely used in food, ayurvedic and cosmetic preparations.

## 3. Basis & Presumptions

1. Production capacity is 0.159375 units/hr over a 8-hour shift for 300 working days per annum; first year is taken at 40% capacity utilisation.
2. Capacity utilisation is assumed at 40%, 50%, 60%, 65%, 70% in years 1–5 respectively.
3. Wastage of 0% is considered on raw material to arrive at net output.
4. Raw-material cost is taken as 60% of revenue; sale price is Rs. 84,708 per unit.
5. Sales and raw-material rates are escalated at 0.0% per year; labour, staff and power costs at 5.0% per year.
6. Raw-material stock is held for 15 days and finished goods for 7 days; debtors are given 30 days and creditors allow 7 days.
7. Working-capital margin is 25.0% (the balance is bank finance).
8. Depreciation is on the WDV method — plant & machinery at 15.0% and miscellaneous assets at 15.0%.
9. Interest on term loan and working capital is taken at 10.5% p.a.; the term loan runs 60 months with a 6-month moratorium.
10. Income tax is provided at 30.9% of profit before tax. Salary and wage rates are as per current market. Premises are owned.
11. Means of finance follows the PM-FME structure — Credit-linked: PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PM-FME) subsidy (35%) + promoter (10%) + term loan (balance).

## 6. Limitations of the Model DPR

i. This DPR provides the basic standard components and methodology for a proposal; it is not a substitute for the entrepreneur's own due diligence.

ii. Entrepreneur-, crop-, and location-specific details (business constitution, project location, raw-material sourcing, SWOT, detailed market research, project rationale for the specific location, community benefit, and employment generation) must be added by the FPO.

iii. The projections rest on assumptions about cost, prices, interest, capacity utilisation and output recovery that vary by place, market and situation; actual figures will differ and the calculations change accordingly.

iv. The means of finance shown (subsidy / promoter contribution / term loan) follows the selected scheme's structure; a different scheme or a credit-linked variant changes the numbers without changing the methodology.

The entrepreneur must verify machinery quotes by demonstration, ensure reliable raw-material supply, maintain optimum inventory, keep a sound debt-equity ratio, and provide all documents required by the sanctioning authority.
